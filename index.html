ENTRY SIGNALS

How to Enter Trades

  • 1. Golden Cross Buy:

    When the 50-day SMA crosses above the 200-day SMA. Signals a major institutional bull market cycle.

  • 2. Swing Momentum Buy:

    Price is trading above the 200-day SMA, and the 20-day SMA crosses above the 50-day SMA. High-probability swing setup with macro momentum.

  • 3. Pullback Bounce Buy:

    In a stacked uptrend (20 > 50 > 200), the price pulls back near the 50-day SMA support and then closes back above the 20-day SMA.

EXIT SIGNALS

How to Protect Capital

  • 1. Trailing Momentum Exit:

    When closing price breaks below the 20-day SMA. Fast signal used by swing traders to lock in gains.

  • 2. Swing Breakdown Exit:

    When the 20-day SMA crosses below the 50-day SMA. Indicates intermediate momentum has reversed.

  • 3. Death Cross / Macro Stop Exit:

    When the 50-day SMA crosses below the 200-day SMA or price breaks down under the 200-day SMA. Hard cut to avoid bear markets.
